Foundations in port operations

TAS begins in stevedoring and port operations - working vessels, wharves and cargo at the waterfront. Operational discipline learned at the quayside remains the group's foundation.

Agency era

TAS Agency & TAS Maritime

Ship agency and marine services formalise the group's waterfront capability - vessel arrival, port clearance, husbandry and marine support for principals calling Malaysian ports.
